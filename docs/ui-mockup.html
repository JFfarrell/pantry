Journey

The whole product is one chain: capture a recipe, pick some, get one shopping list, shop it.

Share or paste a recipe link→ Check and save→ Browse and compare→ Choose recipes for the shop→ Generate the list→ Walk it item by item

Design rules that show up everywhere

Indicators never rely on colour alone (F7, F13)

● In season ◐ Partial ○ Out of season ⓘ Estimated figures ✓ Nutrition complete

Each carries a distinct shape, border style and text label.

Absence is shown, not hidden

No image, no cooking time, no yield or an ingredient without a quantity each get an explicit, honest state. Nothing is replaced by a made-up value.

Errors carry no recipe content

Error messages never repeat a title, ingredient line, link or page text. They say what went wrong and what to do next.

Everything lives on the phone

No account, no cloud. Browsing, search, filtering, detail and selection all work with the network off. Only importing a link, and optionally shopping on tesco.ie, need it.
